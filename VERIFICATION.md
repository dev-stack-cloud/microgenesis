# MICROGENESIS verification

## Build and gameplay

- `npm run build` passed strict TypeScript and produced the Vite bundle in `dist/`. The production preview loaded and played in Chrome 157.0.8103.79.
- `npm test` passed 8 tests. Coverage includes deterministic replay, nursery resources, bonding and mutation, branch damage and regeneration, save continuity, an invalid Crown save, actual Predator energy transfer, and the final encounter.
- The default-seed input policy played full cultures at the production 60 Hz simulation step. It used normal movement, absorption, bonding, mutations, impulse and discharge. All three cultures reached tier III and won. Crystalline finished in 602.62 s, Symbiote in 605.98 s, and Predator in 612.97 s. The runs were accelerated outside the browser.
- `npm run test:browser` passed 4 Chromium scenarios. Tests checked page exceptions and browser console errors. None were reported in the passing run.

| Chromium scenario | Observed behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Desktop 1440 × 900 | Title and field guide, mouse absorption, E bonding, Tab tree, Symbiote choice, D movement and mouse aim, Space/Q, pause, graphics backing resolution, mute, save, reload, resume |
| Crown encounter | A save generated by the full replay loaded as a developed Crystalline III organism. Real pointer movement and Q destroyed the Crown and played the ending. The victory screen and persistent win counter appeared. |
| Defeat | A deliberately lethal acid-pool fixture caused death through the simulation. The result screen and new-culture restart worked. |
| Mobile emulation | A 390 × 844 touch viewport tested absorption, bonding, Predator selection, a joystick drag, impulse and discharge. The paused game resized to 320 × 568. Canvas dimensions and dialog widths were checked. |

The accelerated simulation covered each full culture from inoculation to victory. Chromium tested the opening interactively and resumed a checkpoint from the uninterrupted deterministic run for the last shot. A separate focused Chromium input and audio check exercised diagonal keyboard input, pinch, Web Audio activation, output signal, the voice cap, and mute.

## Frame pacing

Sampled 120 consecutive `requestAnimationFrame` intervals per case on the production build. Chrome used an NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1080 through ANGLE and Direct3D 11. These frame intervals include display scheduling.

| Scenario | Canvas backing | Median | 95th percentile | Longest |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Nursery, 1440 × 900 desktop, High | 1440 × 900 | 13.3 ms | 13.5 ms | 13.6 ms |
| Crown, 1440 × 900 desktop, High | 1440 × 900 | 13.3 ms | 13.5 ms | 14.8 ms |
| Crown, 390 × 844 emulated touch, Adaptive | 355 × 768 | 13.3 ms | 13.5 ms | 13.5 ms |

The recorded desktop cases sustained roughly 75 frames per second in these short measurements. Forced software WebGL was substantially slower during the refinement passes. Physical phones, additional GPUs, Firefox, and Safari were outside verification. Difficulty and art direction were assessed from captured runs and screenshots. Independent human playtesting remains unverified.
